ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLE

The database must become the source of truth.

Inventory should no longer depend on simple JavaScript counters.

The future goal is:

Worker 1 books available inventory
Worker 2 sees that inventory as unavailable
Worker 2 books the next available inventory
Worker 3 and Worker 4 do the same safely

No duplicate bookings.
No overselling.
No stale inventory.
No shared-memory race conditions.
